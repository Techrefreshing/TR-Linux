# Privacy statement

Effective: 1 September 2026

TechRefreshing Linux does not add mandatory telemetry, advertising tracking,
or a TechRefreshing user account. TechRefreshing does not receive local files,
passwords, device details, or application activity merely because the system
is installed or used.

Network-enabled applications and services may contact their own providers.
Examples include Debian package mirrors, Flatpak remotes, Firefox search and
update services, firmware update services, time synchronisation, network
connectivity checks, and websites opened by the user. Those services are
governed by their providers' privacy policies.

Crash reports, logs, and support information are not sent to TechRefreshing
automatically. If a user voluntarily submits logs or screenshots through
GitHub or a support channel, they should remove passwords, usernames, IP
addresses, serial numbers, and other sensitive information first.
